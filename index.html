A quiet home for loud feelings.

Cards get boxed up, fade, or go missing. CardAgain keeps them close, organized, and alive.

Photograph and file
Snap the cards you want to keep and file them by sender, occasion, and date. Your collection stays in step across your devices.
Hear them read aloud
From a short voice sample, CardAgain recreates a familiar voice and reads any card's message aloud.
Handwriting, made readable
Scan a card and its handwriting is turned into text. Faded ink can be sharpened so it's easy to read again.
Keep real recordings
Record someone reading their own card, or your note about the day it arrived, and keep it with the card itself.
Remember the occasions
Birthdays, anniversaries, and holidays sit beside your cards, with a reminder when the day comes back around.
Share it onward
Pass a card to the person it mentions, or to family far away, with a private link or a QR code they can scan.
Private by design
Your cards and voices belong to you. They're stored securely, synced to your account, and never shared without your say. Read the privacy policy

Her handwriting on the envelope, her voice reading it back, the date she pressed it into my hand. All of it, still here.

Three steps, a few minutes.

  1. Photograph the card

    Add a photo and a few details: who it's from and what it was for.

  2. Add a voice

    Upload a short recording of a loved one. Their voice can then read any card you save.

  3. Return to it anytime

    Browse your collection, listen again, and get reminded when the occasion comes back around.
