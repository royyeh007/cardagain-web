What it does

A quiet home for loud feelings

Cards get boxed up, fade, or go missing. CardAgain keeps them close, organized, and alive.

Capture and organize

Photograph the cards you want to keep and file them by sender, occasion, and date. Everything stays in one place, on all your devices.

Hear them read aloud

From a short voice sample, CardAgain recreates a familiar voice and reads any card's message aloud. Handwriting becomes something you can listen to.

Attach real recordings

Record someone actually reading their card, or your own note about the day it arrived, and keep it with the card itself.

Remember the occasions

Birthdays, anniversaries, and holidays live alongside your cards, so the dates that matter never slip by unnoticed.

Private by design

Your cards and voices belong to you. They're stored securely, synced to your account, and never shared without your say.

Simple to share

Pass a card on to the person it mentions, or to family far away, with a private link that opens right in their browser.
