Official rules

No purchase necessary. A purchase or subscription does not improve your chances of winning. Void where prohibited.

1. Sponsor

The CardAgain New Year Giveaway is run by Triple Dynamic Solutions LLC, Illinois, United States, the maker of CardAgain ("we"). Questions: support@cardagain.me.

2. Who can take part

Legal residents of the 50 United States and the District of Columbia who are 18 or older and have a CardAgain account. Employees of Triple Dynamic Solutions LLC and members of their households are not eligible.

3. Dates

The giveaway starts at 12:00 a.m. Central Time on December 20, 2026 and ends at 11:59 p.m. Central Time on January 20, 2027.

4. How to take part

  1. Follow the official CardAgain account on Instagram (@cardagainn).
  2. Add the CardAgain New Year card to your CardAgain account, from this page or from the giveaway banner on the app's Home screen. Adding it is free and it does not count toward your card limit.
  3. Share your New Year card from the app, by link or by QR code.

5. How shares are counted

You earn one share each time a new CardAgain user adds your New Year card to their account during the giveaway. A new user is a person who created their CardAgain account during the giveaway dates. Each person counts once for you, however many times they add the card. Adding the card yourself is how you enter; it is not a share.

Shares to accounts you control, to duplicate or fake accounts, or made by automated means do not count, and we may disqualify anyone we reasonably believe has tried to manipulate the count. Shares are counted from CardAgain's own records, which are final. There is no public ranking.

6. Winners and prizes

The four eligible participants with the most shares when the giveaway ends each receive one $25 Amazon.com Gift Card. The total value of all prizes is $100. If two participants have the same number of shares, the one who reached that number first ranks higher. One prize per person.

Each prize is delivered as a card shared privately to the winner's CardAgain account. Prizes cannot be exchanged for cash; we may substitute a prize of equal or greater value. Amazon.com Gift Cards are subject to Amazon's own terms.

7. Contacting winners

We message each winner in the app, under Contact Us → Your messages, within 7 days of the end of the giveaway. To claim the prize, reply within 7 days with your Instagram username so we can confirm that you follow the official CardAgain account, and confirm that you meet the eligibility rules above.

If a winner is not following the account when we check, does not reply in time, or is not eligible, the prize passes to the next eligible participant.
